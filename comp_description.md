One day. Real problems. Real prizes. Pick a track, form a team of up to 5 (or go solo), and build
something using ClickHouse + Postgres — OLAP and OLTP, like PB&J. This is an AI hackathon grounded
in problems that affect everyday people:

Kids that can not read: the reading crisis Two-thirds of 4th graders read below grade level, and the
kids furthest behind are falling fastest. Build the tool that helps a teacher spot who's quietly
struggling, helps a student find the book they'll actually finish, or helps a library know what's
working

### Ideas

Teacher: “Help me quietly spot kids who are slipping or hiding, before report cards or high‑stakes
tests.”

Student: “Help me find a book I can actually finish and feel good about, not just one more
assignment I’ll abandon.”

Librarian / literacy coordinator: “Help me see which books, series, or programs are actually moving
the needle on reading time and completion, not just circulation counts.”
